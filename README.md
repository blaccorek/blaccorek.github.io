# Tsiorintsoa Andriamihamina — CV & portfolio

The personal site of Tsiorintsoa Andriamihamina, a DevOps consultant and
full-stack engineer. It is published at
[blaccorek.github.io](https://blaccorek.github.io).

## Why it exists

A PDF CV goes stale the moment it is sent. This site is the always-current
version: one place where clients, recruiters and peers can see what I do, where
I have done it, and the tools I have used along the way.

## What a visitor finds

- **What I do** — the domains I work in: GitOps-driven Kubernetes platforms,
  infrastructure as code, CI/CD, observability and reliability, and the product
  engineering that goes with them.
- **Work experience** — each mission with its context, the team I joined, what I
  delivered, and the technologies involved.
- **Education** — degrees and training.

## Goals

- **Accurate**: the content is the CV, kept up to date as missions start and
  end.
- **Fast and dependable**: a fully static site with nothing to go down.
- **Accessible**: readable by everyone, on any device, in light or dark mode.
